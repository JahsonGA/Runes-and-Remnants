Manufacturing

Turning raw materials into a mundane item. You need three things: materials, a tool, and time.

  1. Materials Roughly ⅓ of the item's value. In this campaign the gold cost is replaced by the monster parts and equipment the modification actually needs — the table is a guideline, not a price list.
  2. Tool Each item type needs a tool, and each tool uses a set ability. No proficiency? You can still try, at disadvantage — unless a book or a proficient companion guides you.
  3. Time Spent on the item, though it need not be continuous.
  4. Manufacturing check Rolled at the end of the crafting time. Success completes the item; failure yields a non-functioning one, or a working one with flaws.
Manufacturing check = 1d20 + tool ability + proficiency (if any)
