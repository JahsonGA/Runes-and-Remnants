The trade-off

You framed this as build-vs-stop, but both options skip the step that actually serves your stated goals. Two of your three success criteria are already met — the module works at your table for the first time (harvest crashed on every run until this session), and the portfolio artifact exists (103 tests, 6 CI workflows, pure-logic architecture). The third, real user adoption, is blocked by distribution, not features: the module is not listed on Foundry's package directory, so ~4 hours of listing work moves it further than the ~95 hours in Phases 4–6, which move it by zero. What you give up by continuing to Phase 6 is roughly 133 hours against a ~15% completion probability. The strongest case against this verdict is the Bull's: the 9-month gap was caused by a build that crashed on every harvest, not by lack of capacity — and that cause was just removed, so judging future velocity by that period judges the wrong thing. It doesn't carry the day because it is an untested hypothesis, not because it's implausible — and the verdict costs it nothing, since tripwire 3 gives it 60 days to prove itself before the roadmap is re-underwritten.

What the committee found

Bull

  • Build-vs-stop is a false binary — auto-release means shipping costs one version bump, and was never the alternative to continuing.
  • The 9-month gap has a mechanical cause (every harvest crashed), not a motivational one — and that blocker is now gone.
  • Differentiation lives in Phases 5–6 (runes, evolution); stopping at v1.0 ships only the part that is a weaker clone of Heliana's.

Bear

  • Demonstrated velocity is ~1/25th of what the roadmap needs: 6 commits in 9 months vs 18/week during the initial burst.
  • Zero discovery channel — adoption is blocked by an unfinished submission form, not by missing features.
  • Deferred double-grant bug lets any client duplicate loot: a defeated premise in a scarcity-themed module.

Allocator

  • ~133 hrs to finish; at the revealed 1.8 hrs/wk that is ~14 months base case, ~15% chance of reaching Phase 6.
  • Criteria 1 and 3 are already paid at 0 further hours; criterion 2 needs ~4 hrs of listing, not 95 hrs of features.
  • Same hours spent on a second shipped module or on actually running the campaign beat the roadmap on all three criteria.
